    A NOTE ON SOURCES
Lyricsify is a little window, not a lyrics archive.

    
Searches use LRCLIB and Lyrics.ovh. Availability depends on each source. We don’t store a central lyrics catalog; saved songs stay in this browser.

    
Album artwork is found via Apple’s iTunes Search API and remains the property of its artists, labels, and rights holders.

    
Share cards are generated on your device. Social networks control their own posting and sharing features: X opens a prepared post, while Instagram sharing depends on your device’s share menu.
